🧭 The Rescue Loop

Find → approach → rescue → carry → evacuate.

Walk onto a survivor's tile and a prompt appears — press E or tap Rescue to start pulling them free. Once rescued, they're marked as carried (shown as a small badge on the robot) and count toward your total. You still need to reach the 🏠 safe zone after rescuing everyone to actually complete the mission.

⚠️ Hazards

🧱 Obstacle — blocks movement, doesn't harm you
🔥 Fire — costs a heart; spreads to nearby tiles over time on fire missions
⚡ Electric hazard — costs a heart on contact
☠️ Instant-fail zone — ends the mission the moment you touch it (Expert missions only)
🚗 Patrolling car — drives back and forth along a lane; costs a heart if it hits you
🕵️ Hunter — actively moves toward you every tick, appears on later missions
🌙 Low visibility — a fog layer hides most of the board except a radius around you
⏱️ Time Attack — noticeably shorter mission clock

Your health starts at ❤️❤️❤️ (3 hearts). Lose them all — or touch a skull hazard — and the mission ends. Watch the timer too: it flashes red and beeps once you're under 10 seconds.

💰 Gold & the Shop

Gold coins 🪙 are scattered on some missions. Collect them during a run and, if you complete the mission, that gold is banked permanently. Gold is a one-time reward per mission — if you fail, any gold collected that attempt is lost, but rescuing progress and unlocked missions are never lost. Replaying a mission you've already claimed gold on won't pay out again.

Spend banked gold in the 🛒 Shop (from the start screen) on permanent upgrades:

🛡️ Armor Plating — +1 max health per level (stacks twice)
🛟 Shield Charm — absorbs the first hit each mission for free
🌙 Vision Boost — widens the fog radius on low-visibility missions
🧲 Coin Magnet — auto-collects gold one tile away
⏩ Rescue Tool — cuts the rescue timer roughly in half
🔋 Battery Pack — +10 seconds of mission time per level
🧯 Fire Shield — one free save from fire damage per mission

You can also unlock and equip robot skins in the shop — each one swaps every animation (idle, walk, rescue, damage, victory) to a matching set of emoji.

💔 Second Chances

If you go down mid-mission, you may be offered the chance to spend gold to refill your health and keep playing from exactly where you fell — once per mission. This isn't offered if the mission clock simply runs out, since more health won't buy you more time.

🗺️ Missions

There are 50 missions across three tiers — 🟢 Beginner, 🟡 Intermediate, and 🔴 Expert. Missions open one by one: clearing a mission unlocks the next. Every mission has its own name and briefing, and later missions bring more survivors, more hazards, and less time on the clock. Before each run you'll see a full mission briefing (location, objective, time, danger, reward) followed by a 3-2-1-GO countdown.

Mission-select icons tell you what to expect: 🔥 fire spreads · 🌙 low visibility · 🚗 patrolling cars · ☠️ instant fail · ⏱️ time attack · 🕵️ hunter.

🏅 Rank & Badges

Your rank climbs automatically as you unlock more missions: Rookie Rescuer → Field Rescuer → Emergency Specialist → Elite Rescuer → Master Rescuer. Check your 🏅 Profile from the start screen for your current rank, lifetime stats, and badges earned for things like your first rescue, saving 10+ survivors, finishing a mission without taking damage, and more.

🗺️ Minimap

The small panel in the corner of the board shows a bird's-eye view: 🤖 you, 🟢 survivors, 🔴 danger (hazards, cars, hunters), and 🟡 gold — handy for planning a route before you commit to it.
